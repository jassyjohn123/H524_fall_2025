=== SLIDE 1 ===
Regression and Correlation
Regression Analysis: Statistical procedures for examining relationship between response variable (y) and predictor variables (x’s)
Simple linear regression (SLR) treats situation where there is only one predictor, and both x and y are continuous.
Interest in:
The statistical significance of predictor
The strength and direction of the trend between predictor and response, provided the trend is linear 
Mean response values for the subpopulation of all individuals that have a common predictor value 
Prediction of a future response

Pearson Correlation
The population correlation coefficient is denoted by ρ

To make x and y comparable so that we can define a measure of correlation between them, we standardize the random variables x and y in order to put them on the same scale and to make them dimensionless.

=== SLIDE 23 ===
Correlation vs. Causation
Correlation only provides information about an association 
Care MUST ALWAYS be used in interpreting a correlation 
Correlation does not imply causation 
E.g. Ice cream sales and crime.
Establish causation, use RCT (randomized control trial)
Double blinded.
