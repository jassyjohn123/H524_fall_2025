=== SLIDE 7 ===
The Empirical rule
The empirical rule states that for any normal curve:
68% of the observations lie within 1 SD of the mean. The interval (μ − σ, μ + σ)contains 68% of the data.
95% of the observations lie within 2 SDs of the mean. The interval (μ − 2σ, μ + 2σ) contains 95% of the data. 
99.7% of the observations lie within 3 SDs of the mean. The interval (μ − 3σ, μ + 3σ) contains 99.7% of the data.

=== SLIDE 8 ===
The standard normal distribution
The standard normal distribution is the normal distribution with mean 0 and standard deviation 1. 
So the N (0, 1) is called the standard normal distribution. We typically use Z to denote a random variable that has a standard normal distribution.
It is centered at 0 and places most of its area between -2 and 2 (note empirical rule)

Solution

=== SLIDE 16 ===
Solution
P(60 < S < 85) = P[(60-70)/10 < Z < (85-70)/10] = P(-1 < Z < 1.5) = P(Z < 1.5) – P(Z < -1)
We find: P(Z < 1.5) = 0.933, P(Z < -1) = 0.159.
So P(60 < S < 85) = 0.933 – 0.159 = 0.774.
