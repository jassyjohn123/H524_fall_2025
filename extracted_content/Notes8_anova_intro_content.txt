=== SLIDE 7 ===
ANALYSIS OF VARIANCE
The t-test compares the means of two independent (or dependent) samples and provides a test. The p-value is basically an index of how likely (or unlikely) a result is if the null hypothesis is true. 
A natural extension arises when there are more than two treatments. For example, one may wish to compare a control, a low dose, a medium dose and a high dose of a treatment.
