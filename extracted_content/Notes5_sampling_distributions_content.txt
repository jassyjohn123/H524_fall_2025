=== SLIDE 7 ===
Confidence interval
If procedure is repeated over and over, on average 95% of intervals obtained will contain true parameter value.
We CANNOT say that there is a 95% probability that the parameter is within the confidence limits.  Parameters are not random. (We can say this if we use Bayesian methods.)
