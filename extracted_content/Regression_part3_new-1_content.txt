=== SLIDE 16 ===
Extra Sums of Squares F Test
F-test based on the “extra sums of squares”.
Assuming the reduced Model is nested within the full Model
H0: the reduce model fits data better
    H1: the full model fits data better
F ~ F distribution, with df = (DFEreduced-DFEfull, DFEfull)
SSEfull = Error sum of squares from the full model
SSEreduced = Error sum of squares from the reduced model
DFEfull = Error degrees of freedom from the full model 
DFEreduced = Error degrees of freedom from the reduced model
Both models must be based on the same N
