Anorexia Dataset — Statistical Analysis Summary

Methods

We analyzed weight change pre- to post-treatment across treatment groups
in the anorexia dataset. Descriptive statistics were computed by group.
Within-group effects were tested using paired t-tests (post vs. pre).
Between-group differences were examined using Welch t-tests on change
scores comparing each treatment to Control and reporting Hedges’ g.
Assumptions were assessed via Shapiro–Wilk tests of normality on change
scores and Levene’s test (median-centered) for homogeneity of variance.
We additionally fit an ANCOVA model (Post weight ~ Pre weight +
Treatment) with Control as the reference to estimate adjusted treatment
effects and a Type II ANOVA to quantify the overall treatment effect;
partial eta-squared is reported.

Interpretation Notes

• Positive mean change indicates weight gain post-treatment.
Within-group paired t-tests evaluate whether each group’s average change
differs from zero. Between-group Welch tests compare mean change in each
treatment to Control, robust to unequal variances, and Hedges’ g
quantifies standardized effect size.
• ANCOVA adjusts post-treatment weight for baseline weight, estimating
treatment effects as adjusted differences from Control. Partial
eta-squared indicates the proportion of residual variance in post weight
uniquely explained by treatment after accounting for baseline.
