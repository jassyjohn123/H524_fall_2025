Presentation Day Reminders

What to Bring

What to Submit (by presentation day)

Timing Tips

  • Aim for 10 minutes exactly
  • Practice with a timer beforehand
  • Have a plan if you finish early (extra examples ready)
  • Know what to cut if running long
  • Deduction: -5 points if >13 minutes or <8 minutes

Technical Setup

  • Arrive 5 minutes early to test your laptop connection
  • Have slides in multiple formats (PowerPoint, PDF)
  • Test any videos or animations beforehand
  • Have a backup plan if technology fails

Presentation Order Guidelines

Each team will: 1. Set up (during previous Q&A, ~1 minute) 2. Present (10 minutes) 3. Answer questions (2 minutes) 4. Transition to next team while questions are being asked

Evaluation

Your presentation will be evaluated on: - Organization (3 pts) - Logical flow, clear structure - Time Management (2 pts) - Within 10-12 minutes - Engagement (3 pts) - Professional delivery, equal participation - Visual Aids (2 pts) - Clear, professional slides

Plus your content (question, method, AI comparison, interpretation, reproducibility) for remaining 90 points.

Peer Feedback (Optional)

During presentations, you may be asked to provide brief peer feedback: - One thing the team did particularly well - One constructive suggestion

This helps everyone learn and is not used for grading.

Q&A Tips

Be prepared to answer: - “Why did you choose this statistical method?” - “How did the two AIs differ in their approach?” - “What would you do differently next time?” - “How did you verify the AI’s statistical advice?” - “What was the most challenging part?” - “What practical implications do your results have?”

Special Accommodations

If your team needs special accommodations (e.g., remote presentation, extended time due to disability), please contact the instructor by Week 8.
